## 3. Career Roadmap (High Level)

My roadmap combines several paths captured across my planning documents:[file:1]

**Phase 1 – 0–1 year: Pivot & Foundations**

- Complete CompTIA Security+ and a structured 90‑day SOC skills plan.
- Build small SOC labs and document them (log analysis, alert triage, incident reports).
- Apply for:
  - Junior / Tier‑1 SOC Analyst or Cybersecurity Technician roles.
  - IT Support roles with a security angle.
  - Entry Security Systems / Low‑Voltage Technician or BAS helper roles when they support the OT side.[file:1]

**Phase 2 – 1–3 years: First Technical Roles**

- Cyber track: SOC Tier‑1 analyst focusing on monitoring, detection, and incident workflows.[file:1]
- Field/BAS track: Security systems or low‑voltage technician, or BAS Technician Level 1 (CCTV, alarms, access control, basic automation).[file:1]
- Keep documenting tickets, incidents, and projects (sanitized) into this repo and the portfolio.[file:1]

**Phase 3 – 3–5 years: Specialization**

- Cyber: progress toward more advanced analyst roles and cloud/EDR skills.
- BAS/controls: deepen Niagara/BACnet/HVAC controls knowledge; gain exposure to PLC/SCADA.
- OT: move into roles closer to OT networks and industrial control systems.[file:1]

**Phase 4 – 5–10 years: Hybrid Cyber–OT / Senior Roles**

- OT / ICS Security Analyst or Specialist.
- SCADA/ICS Cybersecurity roles.
- Senior BAS / Controls roles with a strong security focus.[file:1]
